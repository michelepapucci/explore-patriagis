# Armor of Resistance
*Rare (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Armor%20of%20Resistance.webp#right)


You have [Resistance](rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to one type of damage while you wear this armor. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.
