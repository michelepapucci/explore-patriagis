# Cloak of Protection
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Cloak%20of%20Protection.webp#right)


You gain a +1 bonus to [Armor Class](rules/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb.md) and saving throws while you wear this cloak.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 245. Available in the Free Rules (2024)*
