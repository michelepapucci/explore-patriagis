# Periapt of Health
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Periapt%20of%20Health.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this pendant, you can take a [Magic](rules/actions.md#Magic) action to regain `dice:2d4+2|noform|avg` (`2d4 + 2`) [Hit Points](rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md). Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have [Advantage](rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws to avoid or end the [Poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition while you wear this pendant.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284*
