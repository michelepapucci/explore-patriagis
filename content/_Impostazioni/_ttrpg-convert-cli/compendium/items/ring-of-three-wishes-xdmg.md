# Ring of Three Wishes
*Legendary*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Ring%20of%20Three%20Wishes.webp#right)


While wearing this ring, you can expend 1 of its 3 charges to cast [Wish](compendium/spells/wish-xphb.md) from it. The ring becomes nonmagical when you use the last charge.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 296. Available in the Free Rules (2024)*
