# Wand of Secrets
*Uncommon*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Wand%20of%20Secrets.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 3 charges and regains `dice:1d3|noform|avg` (`1d3`) expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can take a [Magic](rules/actions.md#Magic) action to expend 1 charge, and if a secret door or trap is within 60 feet of you, the wand pulses and points at the one nearest to you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 322*
